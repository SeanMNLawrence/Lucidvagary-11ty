*Written at Miller Park, near Gary, Indiana — the site of the author's hemorrhagic stroke.*

The city, a world of constant activity,
Finds its cohesion in the cacophony.
But this hostel, on a numinous road,
Gives the illusion of floating on water.
A sharp spire rises, a monolithic golem,
A silent giant born of steel and loam.
A Leviathan stands guard among the two
Tallest rivals, piercing the hazy blue.
And who maintains this fragile stage?
We can only envisage the unseen flow—
A million hearts that beat within the cage,
A human pulse that makes the giants grow.
