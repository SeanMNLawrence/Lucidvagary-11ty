It wasn't a fading, but a shatter.
The dam behind the eyes gave way—
a sudden, silent sanguineous bloom that
washed the world and thought away.
A thunderclap with no sound, just the fierce,
internal weather of a star collapsing inward,
a world un-made altogether.
